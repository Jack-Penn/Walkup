A QUICK HELLO

Home sounds good on you.

A few small steps, and you're ready.

  1. 01
    Drop in a sound.

    Upload a file and it is selected for you. Set the start, length, and optional fade-in and fade-out; 0 means off.

  2. 02
    Give it a sound check.

    Choose “This browser” to preview, or “Server speaker” to play from your Pi.

  3. 03
    Give everyone their own welcome.

    Start the server with a portal password and connect Life360. Sync Circles, choose your saved home place, and use “Use selected” to assign each person a clip of up to 30 seconds. Enable proximity arrivals when you are ready.

PORTAL_PASSWORD='your-password' python3 app.py

Proximity arrivals use fresh Life360 locations, not the door itself. Door sensors, camera recording, and Spotify playback are not included. Keep the server on a trusted network; don't forward its port to the internet.
